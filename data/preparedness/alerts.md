id: alerts
title: Official alerts versus household planning
source: Ready.gov (paraphrased public guidance)
sourceUrl: https://www.ready.gov/alerts
---

Watches, warnings, and emergency messages come from official sources such as the National Weather Service and local emergency management. Wireless Emergency Alerts, NOAA Weather Radio, and local warning systems are the channels to trust over social media rumors.

A watch means conditions are possible; a warning means take action. Evacuate or shelter only according to official orders for your location. A household kit and a modeled plan do not replace those orders.

Sign up for local alerts before severe weather. If phones are down, a battery radio can still carry official updates. Inferred notes from documents are not watches, warnings, or all-clears.
