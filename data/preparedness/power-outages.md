title: Power outages and household dependencies
source: Ready.gov (paraphrased public guidance)
sourceUrl: https://www.ready.gov/power-outages
---

Outages stop lighting, refrigeration, charging, and many well pumps or medical devices. Food in a closed refrigerator may stay cold for a limited time; a freezer that stays shut lasts longer. Throw out perishable food that has been too warm, and do not use a gas stove or generator indoors.

Elevators, electric garage doors, and electric water pumps may stop. Apartment households on higher floors can lose water pressure and stair-free exit options at the same time.

Keep flashlights where people can find them in the dark. If you use a generator, keep it outdoors and away from windows. Backup power does not restore public water, fuel, or cell towers by itself.
