id: floods
title: Flood water, drinking water, and roads
source: Ready.gov (paraphrased public guidance)
sourceUrl: https://www.ready.gov/floods
---

Flooding can cut roads, contaminate tap water, and hide electrical hazards. Turn around, do not drive through flooded roadways. A short stretch of water can hide a washed-out road or stall a vehicle.

If drinking water may be unsafe, use bottled water or follow local boil-water notices. Do not assume the tap is fine because power is on. Wells and municipal plants can fail independently of household electricity.

Move important items out of basements and low floors when a flood watch is in effect. Never walk into moving water. Afterward, stay out of damaged buildings until officials say it is safe, and watch for gas leaks and downed power lines.
