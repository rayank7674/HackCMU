---
id: evacuation
title: Evacuation and shelter decisions
source: Ready.gov (paraphrased public guidance)
sourceUrl: https://www.ready.gov/evacuation
---

Leave when local officials tell you to evacuate. Know more than one driving route, and keep the vehicle fueled if a storm is approaching. If you have no car, identify in advance who can give a ride or which public transit or paratransit still runs.

Take your go-bag, medicines, chargers, and pet supplies. Lock the home if you have time. Tell your out-of-area contact where you are going.

If you are told to shelter in place, stay inside away from windows unless officials later order an evacuation. Public shelter locations change; confirm them from local emergency management, not from rumor. StormReady does not assign official shelter names.
