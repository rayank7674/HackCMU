title: Build a household supply kit
source: Ready.gov (paraphrased public guidance)

A basic household kit is meant to cover several days if stores, power, or roads are limited. Typical contents include water (about one gallon per person per day), non-perishable food, a flashlight, extra batteries, a battery or crank radio, a first-aid kit, copies of key documents, and any prescription medicines.

Kits work only if they are reachable. Keep one at home in a waterproof container, and pack a smaller go-bag if you may have to leave. Check dates on food, water, and medicine twice a year.

Power-dependent medical devices and refrigerated medicines create extra dependencies: a kit without a plan for backup power or a cooler does not keep those items usable during an outage.
