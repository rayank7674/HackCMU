---
id: plan
title: Make a household communication and meeting plan
source: Ready.gov (paraphrased public guidance)
sourceUrl: https://www.ready.gov/plan
---

A written household plan names how people will reach each other if cell networks are busy, where to meet if home is unsafe, and who can help children, older adults, or pets. Include an out-of-area contact because local calls may fail while a distant text or call still goes through.

Practice two meeting places: one near home, and one outside the neighborhood if roads near you are closed. Write down important phone numbers on paper; a dead phone is not a directory.

If someone relies on electricity for medical equipment, the plan should say how they will get backup power or relocate, and who will check on them. A plan that assumes everyone can drive or climb stairs will fail for households with mobility limits.
